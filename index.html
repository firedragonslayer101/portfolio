About Me

I'm an experienced builder, 3D modeler, map designer, game designer, composer, artist, programmer, and have been an on-and-off Roblox developer for 9 years. My programming skills are intermediate and I have experience with developing frameworks in Lua and C++. I am a co-owner of Crush Depth and Raven Borough, and I have contributed to many on-going projects such as Isekai Legends and Cut the Grass Simulator by Rapid Work. Additionally, I have worked on some projects with Aquatica Studios. When working with me, I ask that you provide complete details so that I can depict your mental image as accurately as possible. From broader aspects such as color palettes and layouts to desired user perspectives and gameplay progression. Overall, please ensure to inform me on exactly what you want. I seek to deliver exactly what my employer needs and wants.
